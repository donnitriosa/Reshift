Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview & Zero-Account Policy

Reshift ({{ site_url }}) is a free browser-accessible image and PDF utility. No account registration, login, email address, or personal profile is required or collected.

2. How Uploaded Files Are Processed

  • Images (PNG, JPEG, WEBP): Processed strictly in memory (io.BytesIO in RAM) and streamed directly back to your browser. Image files are never written to disk or permanent storage.
  • PDF Documents: Processed in memory when output size is 10 MB or smaller. Large PDFs (over 10 MB up to 50 MB) are temporarily staged in an isolated runtime directory (workspace/tmp/) solely to stream the download, deleted immediately after response delivery via a background cleanup task, and wiped automatically whenever the server starts (cleanup_tmp()).
  • Zero Inspection & Zero AI Training: Uploaded files are never viewed, shared, sold, archived, or used to train machine learning or AI models.

3. Rate Limiting & Technical Logs

To protect service availability, client IP addresses are evaluated transiently in memory (slowapi) to enforce per-minute rate limits (20 requests/minute for single images; 10 requests/minute for batch image and PDF endpoints).

4. Local Storage & Third-Party Services

  • Theme Preference: Reshift uses browser localStorage (pref-theme) solely to remember your light or dark mode choice. No tracking or advertising cookies are set by Reshift.
  • External Assets: Typography is loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), and an optional floating support widget is loaded from Trakteer (edge-cdn.trakteer.id).
